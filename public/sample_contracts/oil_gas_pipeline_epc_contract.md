# EPC Lump-Sum Turnkey Contract for 48" Natural Gas Transmission Pipeline & Compressor Station
**Specimen Contract based on FIDIC Conditions of Contract for EPC/Turnkey Projects (Silver Book)**

---

## Contract Agreement

This Agreement is made and entered into on **12th May 2023** by and between:
* **The Employer:** National Hydrocarbon Infrastructure & Gas Transmission Company (NHGTC).
* **The Contractor:** Petro-Energy Consortium for Mega-Pipelines & Industrial Plants (PE-CMIP).

The Employer and Contractor have agreed upon the following terms and conditions:

---

### Article 1: Contract Scope of Works
The Contractor shall execute on a Lump-Sum Turnkey (EPC) basis:
1. Complete detailed engineering, route survey, and geotechnical profiling for 120 km of 48-inch high-pressure gas transmission pipeline (API 5L Grade X70 PSL2).
2. Construction and lowering of pipe strings, trenching through normal and rocky mountainous terrains, and 3 trenchless river crossings utilizing Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD).
3. Field joint automated welding in accordance with API 1104, 100% non-destructive testing (NDT by Automated Ultrasonic Testing - AUT and Radiographic Testing - RT), and heat-shrink sleeve joint coating.
4. Construction, mechanical erection, pre-commissioning, and commissioning of one Gas Compressor Station (comprising 3x 25MW gas-turbine driven centrifugal compressors, scrubbers, coolers, and tie-in metering manifolds).
5. Hydrostatic pressure testing of all pipeline sections according to ASME B31.8 and final nitrogen drying/purging to -20°C dew point.

---

### Article 2: Priority of Documents
The documents forming the Contract are to be taken as mutually explanatory of one another. For the purposes of interpretation, the priority of documents shall be in accordance with the following sequence:
1. The Contract Agreement
2. The Particular Conditions of Contract (Part B)
3. The General Conditions of Contract (FIDIC EPC/Turnkey Silver Book 1999/2017)
4. The Employer's Requirements and Engineering Functional Specifications
5. The Contractor's Technical Proposal and Milestone Breakdown Schedule (WBS)
6. The Approved Pricing Bill of Quantities (BoQ) and Milestone Payment Schedule

---

### Article 3: Contract Price & Milestone Payment Terms
1. **Contract Price:** The accepted Contract Amount is **USD 145,000,000 (One Hundred Forty-Five Million US Dollars)**, payable on an EPC Lump-Sum milestone completion basis.
2. **Advance Payment:** An Advance Payment equal to **15% of the Contract Price** shall be disbursed upon submission of an irrevocable, unconditional Bank Guarantee from a first-class rated bank.
3. **Currency Hedging & Foreign Exchange Adjustment:** If statutory foreign exchange rates deviate by more than ±10% from the Base Date rate, the Contractor shall be entitled to an adjustment mechanism as outlined in Particular Conditions Sub-Clause 13.8.
4. **Milestone Interim Certifications:** Progress billings shall be submitted on achieving verifiable engineering, procurement, mechanical completion, and commissioning milestones. The Employer shall pay within 28 days of certification.

---

### Article 4: Time for Completion & Milestone Schedule
The Time for Completion for the entire Works shall be **24 calendar months** from the Commencement Date.

**Key Milestone Deadlines:**
* **Milestone A (Month 6):** 100% Approved for Construction (IFC) detailed engineering packages and procurement commitment of main line pipes.
* **Milestone B (Month 12):** Delivery of 80% line pipes to site and completion of trenching over 70 km.
* **Milestone C (Month 18):** 100% Mainline Welding, complete HDD river crossings, and erection of compressor station heavy equipment.
* **Milestone D (Month 22):** Satisfactory completion of all 12 pipeline hydrotest sections (ASME B31.8) and compressor mechanical run.
* **Milestone E (Month 24):** Nitrogen purging, Ready-for-Startup (RFSU) certificate, and Initial Handover (Taking-Over Certificate).

---

### Article 5: Employer's Obligations & Right of Access
1. **Right of Access to the Site:** The Employer shall grant the Contractor non-exclusive right of access to the entire 30-meter wide Right-of-Way (ROW) corridor within 45 days of the Commencement Date.
2. **Permits & Environmental Approvals:** The Employer shall obtain all statutory environmental clearances, forestry permits, and municipal road crossing authorizations. Delays in obtaining statutory permits constitute an Employer Risk event.
3. **Free-Issue Gas for Commissioning:** The Employer shall provide clean natural gas for functional performance testing and pack gas introduction free of charge at the designated battery limit.

---

### Article 6: Contractor's Technical Obligations & QA/QC
1. **Welding Quality & NDT Repair Cap:** Welding shall strictly comply with API 1104 standards. The cumulative NDT repair rate across any continuous 5-kilometer spread shall not exceed **3.0% of total inspected joints**. If the repair rate exceeds 3.0%, the Contractor must submit a Mandatory Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) plan and re-qualify welders at its own cost.
2. **Hydrostatic Testing:** Pipeline test pressures shall be held at not less than 1.25 times the Maximum Allowable Operating Pressure (MAOP) for a continuous 24-hour monitored period without detectable leakage or pressure collapse.
3. **Environmental & HSE Standards:** The Contractor shall enforce Zero Harm HSE policies (ISO 14001 & ISO 45001), ensuring deep excavation trench shoring, continuous H2S gas monitoring, and mandatory reporting of any Lost Time Injury (LTI) within 4 hours.

---

### Article 7: Delay Liquidated Damages & Extension of Time (EoT)
1. **Liquidated Damages for Delay (LDs):** If the Contractor fails to achieve the Taking-Over Certificate by the Time for Completion, the Contractor shall pay Liquidated Damages of **USD 50,000 per day of delay**. The total amount of Liquidated Damages shall not exceed **10% of the initial Contract Price**.
2. **Extension of Time (EoT) under Sub-Clause 8.4:** The Contractor shall be entitled to an extension of the Time for Completion if delay is caused by:
   - Exceptionally adverse climatic events exceeding 20-year meteorological return thresholds.
   - Unforeseeable physical ground conditions (e.g., uncharted subterranean rock or unmapped high-pressure pipelines).
   - Any delay, impediment, or prevention caused by or attributable to the Employer, its personnel, or its other contractors on site.
3. **Sub-Clause 20.1 Notice Requirement (28-Day Strict Rule):** If the Contractor considers itself entitled to any extension of the Time for Completion or additional payment, the Contractor shall give notice to the Engineer describing the event within **28 days after the Contractor became aware** of the event. If the Contractor fails to give notice within 28 days, the Employer shall be discharged from all liability in connection with the claim.

---

### Article 8: Variations and Contract Modifications
The Employer may initiate Variations at any time prior to issuing the Taking-Over Certificate under Sub-Clause 13. The cumulative aggregate net increase or decrease of the Works shall not exceed **±20% of the Contract Price** without formal mutual contract amendment.

---

### Article 9: Dispute Resolution & Arbitration
1. **Dispute Adjudication Board (DAB):** Any dispute arising from this Contract shall be referred to a 3-member Dispute Adjudication Board (DAB) appointed jointly within 28 days of the Commencement Date.
2. **International Commercial Arbitration:** If either party is dissatisfied with the DAB decision, the dispute shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC, Paris) by three arbitrators appointed in accordance with said Rules. The governing law shall be the substantive law of the Contract jurisdiction.
